Privacy Policy

Sweat Logistics for iPhone · Effective November 9, 2026

Short version: Sweat Logistics does not collect, transmit, sell or share any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

What the app stores

The app saves the following on your device only:

  • Your workouts: exercises, weights, reps, times and how each session felt
  • The name you enter, if any, and your plan start date

This data is kept in the app's local storage on your iPhone. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. It may be included in your own iCloud or computer backups of the device, which Apple handles under its own privacy policy.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data
  • No network requests to our servers. We don't have any.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the rest timer schedules a local notification on your device. It's created and delivered on the phone itself, and no data leaves the device.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual workouts on the Progress tab. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
