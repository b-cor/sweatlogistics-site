Privacy Policy

Sweat Logistics for iPhone · Effective November 25, 2026

Short version: Sweat Logistics does not collect, transmit, sell or share any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone. If you turn on Apple Health, the app saves your workouts to Health and reads your body weight from it, on your iPhone only.

What the app stores

The app saves the following on your device only:

  • Your workouts: exercises, weights, reps, times, warm-up cardio and how each session felt
  • The name you enter, if any, your plan and its start date, and your settings (such as pounds or kilograms and rest times)
  • If you use Sweat Logistics Pro: your own workouts, split and custom exercises

This data is kept in the app's local storage on your iPhone. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. It may be included in your own iCloud or computer backups of the device, which Apple handles under its own privacy policy.

The home-screen widget and the rest timer on the Lock Screen read this data on your iPhone to show your next workout and the time left. Nothing is sent anywhere to do this.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data
  • No network requests to our servers. We don't have any.

Apple Health

Saving workouts to Apple Health is off unless you turn it on (Settings in the app → Save workouts to Health), and iOS asks your permission first. If you allow it:

  • Each finished workout is saved to Health as a strength training workout, with its start and end time and an estimate of the calories it burned.
  • The app reads your most recent body weight from Health, only to estimate those calories. It is not stored or used for anything else.

Health data is handled on your iPhone and is never sent to us or to anyone else. It is not used for advertising, marketing or data mining, and it is not shared with third parties. You can turn this off or remove the app's access at any time in the iPhone Settings app under Health → Data Access & Devices → Sweat Logistics. Workouts already saved stay in Health until you delete them there.

Purchases

The free trial and the Sweat Logistics Pro unlock are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. The app only asks the App Store whether Pro is unlocked on your Apple Account, and when you first downloaded the app, so that people who downloaded it before Pro existed keep everything for free.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the rest timer schedules a local notification on your device. It's created and delivered on the phone itself, and no data leaves the device.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual workouts on the Progress tab. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone. Workouts saved to Apple Health are kept in Health; you can delete them in the Health app. Purchases stay on your Apple Account and can be restored if you reinstall.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
