Sweat Logistics

A simple gym log for a gradual, two-then-three-days-a-week plan.

Getting help

Found a bug or have a question? Open an issue on GitHub and describe what happened. Include your iPhone model and iOS version if you can.

Common questions

Where is my data stored?
Only on your iPhone. The app has no account and no server, and it doesn't sync between devices.
How do I change my name or plan start date?
Tap the gear icon at the top right of the Workout or Plan tab.
Why is the rest timer notification not showing?
Allow notifications for Sweat Logistics in the iPhone Settings app, under Notifications.
How do I delete a workout?
On the Progress tab, swipe left on the workout in the list.
Sweat Logistics is not medical advice. Check with your doctor before starting a new exercise program.
