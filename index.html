Sweat Logistics

A simple gym log for a gradual, two-then-three-days-a-week plan.

Getting help

Found a bug or have a question? Open an issue on GitHub and describe what happened. Include your iPhone model and iOS version if you can.

Common questions

Where is my data stored?
Only on your iPhone. The app has no account and no server, and it doesn't sync between devices.
How do I change my name or plan start date?
Tap the gear icon at the top right of the Workout or Plan tab.
Why is the rest timer notification not showing?
Allow notifications for Sweat Logistics in the iPhone Settings app, under Notifications.
How do I delete a workout?
On the Progress tab, swipe left on the workout in the list.
How do I switch between pounds and kilograms?
Tap the gear icon, then choose under Units. Everything you've logged, including history, is converted, so nothing is lost.

Apple Health

How do I save workouts to Apple Health?
Tap the gear icon and turn on Save workouts to Health, then allow access when iOS asks. Each finished workout is saved as strength training, with calories estimated from its length and your body weight in Health.
Workouts aren't showing up in Health.
Open the iPhone Settings app, go to Health → Data Access & Devices → Sweat Logistics, and turn on Workouts. Only workouts finished after you turn this on are saved.

Sweat Logistics Pro

What's free and what's in Pro?
The guided beginner plan, your history and your progress are always free. Pro adds your own split: the workout editor, split templates and custom exercises.
How does the free trial work?
You can try Pro free for 14 days. It's not a subscription and nothing is charged when it ends. After the trial, Pro is a one-time purchase. If you don't buy it, the app goes back to the beginner plan, and your own workouts and history stay saved for when you unlock it.
I bought Pro but it isn't unlocked, or I have a new iPhone.
Tap the gear icon, then Restore purchases under Pro. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.
I downloaded the app before Pro existed. Do I have to pay?
No. If you downloaded Sweat Logistics before Pro was added, you keep everything for free.
How do I get a refund?
Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
